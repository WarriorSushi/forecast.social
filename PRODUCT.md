# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Forecast.social is for opinionated, online-native people who already make public predictions for status: technology and AI followers, crypto communities, sports fans, and culture obsessives. They want a fast way to put a probability on the record, follow other forecasters, and prove that their confidence was earned. The primary launch audience is 18–34 and mobile-first, with Tech Twitter and Crypto Twitter as the beachhead.

## Product Purpose

Forecast.social turns predictions into a permanent, public track record. Users make probabilistic calls without wagering money; resolved calls compound into an explainable Forecast Score, category reputation, and shareable receipts. The product succeeds when people make repeat predictions, return for resolutions, and share proof of being right. Follower count is context; demonstrated accuracy is the identity.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, intelligent, and candid. The voice is confident without becoming casino-like, memey, or corporate. It celebrates conviction while remaining honest about uncertainty and mistakes. The interface should feel as trustworthy as a financial record and as socially legible as a public profile.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a betting exchange, Bloomberg-terminal cosplay, a crypto casino, or a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page. Avoid neon-on-black, purple gradients, glassmorphism, excessive glow, noisy charts, ornamental editorial layouts, generic card grids, and decorative animation. Do not use gambling mechanics, monetary balances, winnings language, or urgency patterns that pressure users into calls.

## Design Principles

1. The record is the product. Make timestamps, probability history, outcomes, and scoring provenance easy to audit.
2. Reduce the distance to a meaningful first call. Every core screen should present a clear next prediction or social action.
3. Numbers carry the hierarchy. Probabilities, scores, streaks, and time-to-close deserve the strongest visual emphasis.
4. Confidence needs restraint. Use direct copy, precise states, and sparse decoration so the product feels credible.
5. Wins travel, losses remain visible. Sharing should celebrate proven calls without hiding the permanent record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark themes. Every interaction must work with keyboard and touch, retain visible focus states, avoid color-only meaning, respect reduced-motion preferences, and maintain readable contrast for positive and negative outcome colors. The primary mobile target is a 390px viewport.
