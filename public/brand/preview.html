Tagline candidates

T-1 — T-10 · pick one
  1. Be right. Get famous. — existing hero, baseline
  2. Call it. Own it. — short, ownership-flavored
  3. Make the call. Make the record. — action + outcome, parallel
  4. Famous for being right. — outcome-led declarative
  5. Predict in public. — three words, exposes the social pressure
  6. Predict like an oracle. For free. — Temu-shape aspirational
  7. Bet your reputation. Not your money. — sharp positioning vs Polymarket/Kalshi
  8. Loud takes. Public scoreboard. — two-noun rhythm, cocky
  9. The receipts go public. — keeps "receipts" but reframes as exposure
  10. Stake a number. Stack a record. — alliterative, describes the loop

How to pick: tell Claude three things — favicon (e.g. F-A), logo (e.g. L-1), tagline (e.g. T-5). One message, one commit, all three live everywhere they appear.
