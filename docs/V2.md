# V2.md — What's next after Phase 7

This doc captures the v2 build plan. The v1 phases (0–7) are complete and shipped; the product can sign people up, accept predictions, resolve markets, score forecasters, surface a feed, render share cards. From here, the highest-leverage focus is on **content supply** — the bottleneck after launch is "what do I predict on today?" and admin can't author markets fast enough.

The post-launch backlog from `IMPLEMENTATION.md` is reordered here by impact + cost.

## V2 build order

### V2.0 — User-submitted markets (shipped)

**Why first:** Markets are the supply side of the marketplace. Today every market comes from `is_admin = true`, which doesn't scale past 50 markets. Letting forecasters propose markets compounds inventory and gives the community ownership over the questions.

**What to ship:**

1. **Schema**
   - Add `market_proposals` table: same columns as `markets` (title, description, category, closes_at, resolves_at, resolution_source) plus `proposed_by`, `status` enum (`pending` | `approved` | `rejected` | `needs_revision`), `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`, `rejection_reason`.
   - Trigger: on `status = approved`, copy the proposal into `markets` and set `markets.created_by = proposed_by` (give the proposer credit on the post-byline).

2. **Server actions**
   - `proposeMarket` — non-admin gated; Zod validates everything `createMarket` validates plus a `rationale` field (why this question, how does it resolve cleanly?).
   - `reviewProposal` — admin-only; transitions status with optional `rejection_reason`. On approval, the trigger does the copy.

3. **UI**
   - `/markets/propose` — same form as the admin create page, with the rationale field. Visible to anyone signed in.
   - `/admin/proposals` — admin queue, sortable by submission time, with quick Approve / Reject buttons and a notes textarea.
   - On the profile, surface "Proposed" markets in a tab next to "Recent calls."
   - Notification: `proposal_resolved` (approved / rejected) to the proposer.

4. **Rate limits**
   - One proposal per user per 6 hours (the friction is the point).

5. **Gamification (light)**
   - When an approved market resolves with at least N predictions, give the proposer a small "market-maker" badge on their profile. Tracked in `users.markets_proposed_approved` / `users.markets_proposed_resolved`.

**Estimated effort:** one phase-equivalent (~1 week if shipped tight).

**Risks:**
- Spam / toxic markets — rate limit + admin queue + clear rejection language.
- Markets the admin can't actually resolve (e.g., "Will X happen by Y?" where Y has no public ground truth). The form should reject markets without a resolution source URL.

### V2.1 — Durable resolution fan-out

**Why next:** Resolution now uses bulk scoring writes and bounded concurrency, and a Vercel Cron recomputes every active forecaster nightly. A market with thousands of predictors can still outgrow one request. Inngest would make that fan-out durable and retryable.

**What to ship:**

1. `pnpm add inngest` (already in TECH_STACK pin).
2. `src/lib/inngest/client.ts` and `src/app/api/inngest/route.ts` (the public webhook handler).
3. Functions:
   - `market.resolve` — replaces the synchronous fan-out; fires `score.recompute` per predictor and a `notify.market.resolved` per predictor.
   - `score.recompute` — single-user, idempotent. Wraps `recomputeUserScore`.
   - Keep the existing authenticated Vercel Cron for nightly decay unless volume requires durable batches there too.
4. `resolveMarket` server action: insert the audit row + flip the market to resolved, then enqueue `market.resolve` and return immediately. Toast says "Resolution queued."

**Estimated effort:** ~2 days. The scoring math doesn't change; we just move the orchestration off the request path.

### V2.2 — Live consensus push via Supabase Realtime (shipped)

**Why third:** Today the consensus on a market page updates only when the page re-renders (revalidatePath after submit). With Realtime, two users watching the same market see the consensus move as new calls come in. This is the social moment.

**What to ship:**

1. A small `<LiveConsensus />` client component on `/markets/[slug]` that subscribes to a Supabase channel `market:<id>:consensus` and updates the displayed consensus + call count.
2. A server-side broadcast in the predictions submit flow that emits the new consensus_probability.
3. Keep the sparkline server-rendered for now; live-update only the headline number to stay simple.

**Estimated effort:** ~1 day.

### V2.3 — Inviting people (invite codes, shipped)

**Why fourth:** Quality-of-signup matters at launch. A 200-code first wave keeps quality high while we observe the product under real traffic.

**What to ship:**

1. `invite_codes` table: `code` (PK, 8 alnum chars), `created_by` (admin), `used_by`, `used_at`, `created_at`.
2. Gate `/sign-up` on a `?code=` param. Invalid / missing / used code → friendly error.
3. `/admin/invites` — admin can generate 50 codes at a time, see who used what.
4. Invite-share affordance on the profile: "Invite a forecaster" reveals a code the user can copy. Each user gets 3 codes when they cross 1500 score (paywalls on talent, not money).

**Estimated effort:** ~1.5 days.

### V2.4 — Email transactional (Resend + react-email)

- Welcome email on signup
- "Your market resolved" email per predictor on resolution
- "Reply on your comment" digest (daily, batched, not per-event spam)
- "You crossed N" milestone email

**Estimated effort:** ~2 days. Mostly templates; the trigger points are already in the codebase as in-app notifications.

### V2.5 — Algorithmic feed v2

The Phase 5 feed is three handwritten lanes. V2.5 introduces light scoring on top: lane items get re-ranked by recency × interest (your category preferences) × diversity. Still no ML; just a deterministic ranker.

**Estimated effort:** ~2 days.

### V2.6 — Public API (read-only)

Open `/api/public/markets`, `/api/public/users/:handle`, `/api/public/scores`. Rate-limited (Upstash, swap from the in-memory limiter). Gives press, researchers, and tinkerers an entry point. Read-only on v2.6 — write API is a v3 question.

**Estimated effort:** ~3 days including the rate-limit infra swap.

## What we are NOT doing in v2

These are listed in IMPLEMENTATION.md "After launch" but are larger investments and unlock less per dollar than the above:

- **Native iOS / Android** — defer. PWA is fine for now. Native is a $200K+ commitment.
- **DMs** — the social product is the public scoreboard. DMs are a different product and a moderation tax we don't need yet.
- **Web push notifications** — in-app notifications are sufficient at this stage. Web push is fragile on iOS and adds infrastructure.
- **Sponsored markets** — premature; we'd need to be the destination first.
- **Creator subscriptions** — also premature; talent has to compound on the network before paid newsletters mean anything.
- **Multi-language** — keep the i18n folder structure ready (see Phase 7 backlog) but don't translate until we have demonstrable demand from a non-English region.

## Order of operations

V2.0 → V2.1 → V2.2 → V2.3 in order; V2.4 / V2.5 / V2.6 in any order after that.
